Personal AI Medical Imaging Project

Advanced Brain Tumor Detection & Analysis

Conducted within the Computer Science department at Universitas Klabat, this research explores the use of deep learning for enhancing brain tumor classification and segmentation accuracy from MRI data.

Project Capabilities

This personal project applies deep learning to medical imaging.

Classification

Automatically identify and classify different types of brain tumors including gliomas, meningiomas, and pituitary.

  • Multi-class tumor detection

Segmentation

Advanced pixel-level segmentation tumor boundaries and measure tumor volume for treatment planning.

  • Boundary detection

Visual Explanation

GradCAM visualization highlights the regions that influence AI decisions, providing transparency and building trust in diagnostic results.

  • Heat map generation
  • Decision transparency

AI Interpretation

Get detailed, human-readable explanations of findings with clinical recommendations and treatment guidance powered by LLM.

  • Clinical reports
  • Risk assessment
  • Treatment suggestions

Tumor Classification Types

Our AI system can identify and analyze multiple types of brain tumors with detailed characteristics and clinical significance.

High Grade

Glioma

Aggressive tumors originating from glial cells. Fast-growing and requiring immediate medical attention and treatment planning.

Growth Rate Rapid
Low Grade

Meningioma

Benign tumors arising from the meninges. Typically slow-growing with good prognosis when detected early.

Growth Rate Slow
Medium Grade

Pituitary

Hormonal tumors affecting the pituitary gland. Can impact multiple body systems through hormonal disruption.

Growth Rate Variable
