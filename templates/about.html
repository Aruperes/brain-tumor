Project Overview

NeuroCare AI is an advanced medical technology system designed to assist healthcare professionals in the early detection and analysis of brain tumors through deep learning–based classification and segmentation.

This project, developed by Revando Aruperes under the supervision of Sir Green Sandag, integrates modern computer vision architectures and explainable AI visualization techniques to enhance interpretability and reliability in medical diagnostics.

The system utilizes state-of-the-art deep learning frameworks such as EfficientNet, ResNet, VGG, and DenseNet, combined with visualization methods like Grad-CAM, enabling transparent diagnostic insights for medical experts. NeuroCare AI represents a step toward improving healthcare technology through the synergy of machine learning and clinical data analysis.

Result and Impact

This project involved training and comparing four distinct deep learning models: EfficientNet, DenseNet, ResNet, and VGG. After a thorough performance analysis, EfficientNet was selected as the primary model for this application due to its superior accuracy, achieving 98.47%.

The integration of Grad-CAM with the high-performing EfficientNet model provides crucial visual feedback. This builds trust and aids in diagnostic verification by showing why the model reached its conclusion.

This project serves as a strong proof-of-concept for how modern AI techniques can be integrated into a practical, user-friendly tool. It supports medical professionals by improving diagnostic speed and is also supported by a generative LLM (Large Language Model) to explain the Grad-CAM results, enhancing research in explainable AI (XAI) in healthcare.
